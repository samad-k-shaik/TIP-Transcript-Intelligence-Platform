Requirement 2: Sentiment Trends & Sentiment Drift

Dynamic Behavioral Analysis

Channel-Specific Trends

  • Customer Support Calls (Highly Volatile): Highly reactive waves. Negative drop indicators correspond to bugs; sharp recoveries represent successful solutions.
  • External Accounts (Forward-Looking): Generally high. Slower, smoother wave structures indicate ongoing client health and new features adoption.
  • Internal Syncs (Consistently Stable): Neutral-to-positive stability proving consistent team focus and healthy roadmap alignment.
